Frequently asked questions

Is my photo safe?

We use your source photo only to generate, deliver, and recover the result you requested. Source uploads and generated assets are kept in private storage and handled as described in our Privacy Policy. We don't build a face database or train our own model on your photo.

Can I try it for free?

Yes: the first preview sticker is free. Upload a photo, pick a style, and see the result before you pay.

What if the stickers don't look like me?

You always see the preview first, and nothing is charged until you're happy with it. If the likeness isn't there, simply don't buy the pack. If something went wrong with a pack you already paid for, write to support with /paysupport in the bot and we'll take a look.

Do I need to type the emotions?

No. In the Mini App, tap the emotions you want in the visual emoji grid: there are 18 to choose from, and each one you pick becomes a sticker. A pack has 6, 12 or 18.

How long does it take?

Usually a few minutes from photo to finished pack; a busy queue can make it longer.

How do I pay and get a refund?

Payment is handled through Telegram Stars inside the bot. If anything goes wrong with a payment, send /paysupport in the bot and we'll review it. Statutory rights still apply.

What kind of photo works best?

For people, use a clear, well-lit photo without heavy filters, dark sunglasses, or covered faces; for a couple or family, keep every face visible. For a pet, make sure its face and defining features are clear and unobstructed.

Who can use StickerKit?

StickerKit is for users aged 18 or older. Using it means you accept the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy. We don't ask for a birth date or ID.

What languages are supported?

This site is available in English, Russian, Spanish, and Brazilian Portuguese. The bot and the Mini App are in English and Russian.

Are there other ways to pay less?

Yes. Post a story about your pack and get 2 more stickers free. Join our Telegram channel and let us show your pack there for 3 more. Do both, and your first 6-sticker pack is free. You can also send an unpaid pack to a friend so they can pay for it as a gift.
