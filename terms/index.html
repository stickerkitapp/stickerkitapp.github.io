The service

StickerKit makes a personalized sticker pack from a photo you upload, using the AI image-generation provider identified in the Privacy Policy, and publishes it as a Telegram sticker set for your Telegram account. Before you pay, you see a preview and receive one sticker from it free of charge; the number of free previews per account may be limited. Some features unlock stickers for free, for example posting a story about your pack or joining our Telegram channel and letting us show your pack there; each is offered once per account.
